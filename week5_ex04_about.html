Our Story & Owner

     

Meet the heart behind Shongololo SA — a journey rooted in heritage, community and Ubuntu.

   
       
     
               
          Did you know? Shongololo means "millipede" in isiZulu — symbolizing how small steps (and legs!) carry big dreams forward. Every product supports rural artisans.       
             
   
       
     
               
         
                       

Thandiwe Nkosi

           

Founder & Creative Director

         
       
     
     
       
         

Dumela! I'm Thandiwe

         

Born in Soweto, raised between Johannesburg and the Eastern Cape — my passion for local crafts started around my grandmother’s kitchen table, watching her weave ilala palm baskets.

         

After studying ethnobotany at the University of Cape Town, I traveled across Limpopo, KZN and the Karoo, meeting extraordinary artisans, rooibos farmers and beaders. In 2019, I founded Shongololo SA to create a platform that tells authentic South African stories through high-quality, ethically sourced products.

         

Our promise: 10% of all profits go back to women-led cooperatives in rural Mpumalanga. We believe in fair trade, sustainable sourcing, and celebrating the beauty of Mzansi.

Our Core Values

   
     
       
                   
           
Ubuntu
           

"I am because we are." We collaborate closely with local communities, sharing profits and uplifting women-led groups.

         
       
     
     
       
                   
           
Sustainability
           

Our packaging is recycled, our rooibos is organic, and our beadwork uses eco-friendly materials.
